How this works

Every module ends with a check. Those answers are stored in your browser and nowhere else, and this page reads them back: a module you scored badly on, or have not seen for a fortnight, is more likely to come up than one you answered correctly this morning. Modules you have never been asked about here get a nudge upwards too, so the deck does not collapse onto your worst three topics.

Questions are the modules' own — the multiple-choice ones where a module has authored questions, and recall prompts built from the page's own key takeaways everywhere else. Nothing is generated at read time, so an answer here cannot drift from the module it came from.

No account, no backend, nothing leaves this device. Clearing your browser storage resets it.
